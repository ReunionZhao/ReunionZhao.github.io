Research Agenda

My question is how instability and heterogeneity at the individual level translate into equilibrium outcomes at the market level. I use structural estimation to make that translation explicit.

My agenda is to measure cognitive states (attention, memory, and beliefs) and endogenize them in equilibrium, treating what people have in mind as a state variable of markets. This state has its own law of motion, which the actions of firms and policy enter.

This question has many manifestations: in credit markets, in household financial decisions such as mortgage refinancing, and in deposit markets, where I am currently trying to introduce cognitive microfoundations into structural models of bank competition, starting with depositor attentiveness.

I discipline these microfoundations with new measurements from survey experiments and LLMs.
